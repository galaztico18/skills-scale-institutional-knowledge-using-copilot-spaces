# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Security / Compliance Lead

### Role Summary
Security / Compliance Leads protect the integrity, privacy, and compliance posture of project work. They help teams identify and mitigate security and regulatory risks before they reach production.

### Responsibilities
- Review architectures, workflows, and data handling for security and compliance risks
- Partner with engineering and PM to define mitigation plans and control requirements
- Coordinate security testing, vulnerability triage, and remediation follow-up
- Confirm release readiness against required compliance or policy checks
- Escalate high-risk issues to the appropriate stakeholders and decision-makers

### Goals
- Reduce security and compliance risk
- Prevent late-stage issues that block release or create customer harm
- Align technical work with policy, privacy, and operational standards

### Typical Communication
- Security review meetings and design checkpoints
- Risk register updates and remediation tracking
- Release-readiness and incident escalation communication

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works with Developers to review code, system design, and threat exposure
- Partners with Project Managers to connect security risks to project plans and milestones
- Advises Product Managers on trade-offs between delivery speed, customer value, and compliance constraints
- Coordinates with QA to ensure security validation is included in acceptance and release checks

---

## Technical Lead / Engineering Manager

### Role Summary
Technical Leads and Engineering Managers guide engineering execution, technical direction, and team capacity. They help translate product goals into practical delivery decisions and keep implementation quality high.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical standards, architecture direction, and implementation trade-offs
- Help prioritize engineering work and manage team capacity and sequencing
- Support developers with technical decision-making and dependency resolution
- Align sprint delivery with product goals and quality expectations
- Identify technical risks early and propose mitigation or escalation paths

### Goals
- Deliver sustainable, maintainable engineering execution
- Balance speed, quality, and technical debt
- Keep delivery teams aligned with product and project priorities

### Typical Communication
- Technical planning and sprint reviews
- Design discussions and architecture decisions
- Coordination with PM and Product on scope, dependencies, and risk

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works closely with Developers on implementation, quality, and technical health
- Supports Project Managers with schedule realism, dependency tracking, and risk management
- Partners with Product Managers to translate roadmap intent into feasible delivery plans
- Provides QA with technical context needed for validation strategy and release readiness

---

## Release Manager

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate the readiness, scheduling, and communication of a release. They help ensure deployments are well planned, tested, and communicated across the organization.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate release windows, deployment sequencing, and rollback planning
- Validate that release criteria, smoke tests, and operational checks are complete
- Partner with PM, QA, and engineering to confirm readiness before launch
- Communicate release status, risks, and stakeholder notices during the release process
- Support post-release verification and incident response coordination if needed

### Goals
- Reduce release risk and operational disruption
- Improve predictability and visibility across teams
- Ensure successful deployment and clear stakeholder communication

### Typical Communication
- Release planning and readiness reviews
- Deployment checkpoints and release notes
- Post-release status reporting and incident updates

### Interaction with existing roles
- Collaborates with Developers and QA on testing completion and deployment readiness
- Works with Project Managers to align release timing with milestones and stakeholder commitments
- Keeps Product Managers informed of launch timing, customer impact, and known risks
- Coordinates with Security / Compliance Lead for policy checks and risk review before production release

---

## Customer Success / Support Liaison

### Role Summary
Customer Success and Support Liaisons connect delivery teams to customer needs, support patterns, and operational feedback. They help keep customer impact visible during planning and execution.

### Responsibilities
- Gather insights from support tickets, customer feedback, and account issues
- Translate customer pain points into product, backlog, or risk inputs
- Coordinate with PM and Product teams on customer-impacting priorities
- Provide context for escalations, service expectations, and customer communication
- Track recurring issues that may require roadmap or process changes

### Goals
- Improve customer outcomes and service quality
- Ensure customer insights inform project decisions
- Reduce friction between support operations and product delivery

### Typical Communication
- Customer feedback reviews and issue triage
- Weekly stakeholder updates and support summaries
- Escalation and incident coordination when customer impact is high

### Interaction with existing roles
- Provides Product Managers with evidence for prioritization and feature refinement
- Shares operational risks and customer pain points with Project Managers for planning and escalation
- Works with Developers and QA to clarify bug severity, user impact, and acceptance scenarios
- Supports release communication and follow-up after launch through stakeholder coordination

---

## Data / Analytics Lead

### Role Summary
Data and Analytics Leads help teams measure progress, customer value, and operational outcomes. They ensure decisions are informed by evidence rather than assumptions.

### Responsibilities
- Define key metrics, dashboards, and tracking plans for product and project health
- Ensure instrumentation, reporting, and quality checks are in place for success measures
- Review trends and outcomes to inform product, delivery, and operational decisions
- Support experimentation, adoption analysis, and post-launch evaluation
- Partner with stakeholders to clarify what success looks like and how it is measured

### Goals
- Make outcomes measurable and visible
- Improve learning cycles through evidence-based decisions
- Connect execution progress to business and customer impact

### Typical Communication
- KPI reviews and dashboard reporting
- Weekly metric check-ins and milestone reviews
- Decision support for roadmap and release evaluation

### Interaction with existing roles
- Helps Product Managers validate whether outcomes match the intended value proposition
- Supports Project Managers with milestone tracking and evidence for status reporting
- Works with Developers and QA to confirm instrumentation and validation signals are reliable
- Shares insights with Customer Success / Support Liaison to connect operational feedback to measurable outcomes
